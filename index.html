Research Experience

2025
Common-Envelope Evolution of Ultralight Boson Clouds
Developed a semi-analytical gravitational-molecule framework for common-envelope evolution in black-hole binaries and found eccentricity growth to O(0.1) in the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA band.
Phys. Rev. D (2026) arXiv
2024
Superradiant Clouds in Close Compact-Object Binaries
Developed analytical models of cloud depletion and resonant transfer in close compact-object binaries. Showed that common-envelope evolution can preserve superradiant clouds into the LISA band and enable non-superradiant objects, such as neutron stars, to host boson clouds.
Phys. Rev. D (2024) arXiv
2023
Tidal Response of Regular Black Holes
Undergraduate thesis: analyzed scalar and vector tidal perturbations of regular black holes, including the Hayward, Bardeen, and Simpson-Visser metrics.
2022
Photons Generated by Gravitational Waves near a Neutron Star
Studied high-frequency gravitational-wave conversion into photons in neutron-star magnetospheres, identifying a possible multimessenger probe of high-frequency gravitational waves and dark matter.
Nucl. Phys. B (2022) arXiv

Code

GRTeclyn

I am part of the GRTL Collaboration team developing GRTeclyn, the successor to GRChombo. It is an open-source numerical relativity code built on AMReX, with adaptive mesh refinement and GPU acceleration, and it is the main tool I use in my research.
